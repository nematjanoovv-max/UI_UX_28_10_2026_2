Our story

Grown slowly,
shipped lovingly

GreenShop started with a windowsill, three cuttings and a stubborn belief that everyone deserves a little more green in their life. Today we grow over 80 varieties in our family nursery — no shortcuts, no plastic wrap, no unhappy plants.

  • Nursery-grown, not imported

    Acclimatised to real homes, so they settle in faster.

  • Watered & checked before shipping

    Every plant gets a health check the morning it leaves us.

  • Plastic-free, always

    Recycled paper pots and compostable packing tape.

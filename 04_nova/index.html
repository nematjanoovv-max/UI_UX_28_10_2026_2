Less is more
at Nova

We strip a project back to what matters: light, proportion, and the way a room makes you feel at 4pm. Then we build it to last fifty years, not five.

Every Nova project is led by one of our four partners from first sketch to final walkthrough. No handoffs, no house style, no filler.

Welcome to Rayal Park

Warm hospitality, endless experiences.

We are a family house, not a chain. That means the person at reception remembers your coffee, the garden is yours after breakfast, and check-out is whenever you finally finish the crossword.

  • Breakfast from the garden, until 11
  • Bikes, books and a very slow record player
  • Twelve rooms — no corridors, just landings

Why you choose us

Easy booking

Book direct in under a minute. No prepayment, no booking fee, free cancellation until 48 hours before.

Breakfast forever

Slow coffee, warm bread and eggs from the farm down the road. Served in the garden when it's sunny.

Room to breathe

High ceilings, real windows and plants that someone actually waters. Every room has a reading chair.

Quiet comforts

Rain showers, linen sheets and a small spa you can book for an hour. Nothing is extra at check-out.

Why organic

Better for the soil, the farmer and you.

01

No pesticides

We farm with compost, crop rotation and a lot of patience. Nothing synthetic touches your food.

02

Fair to growers

Our farmers set a fair price and we pay it. No auctions, no last-minute renegotiation.

03

Less waste

We harvest to order, pack in paper and return the crates. Nothing is wrapped twice.

04

Real seasonality

No strawberries in December. What is ready is what you get — and it tastes better for it.

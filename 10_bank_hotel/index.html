The hotel

Marble, brass and a little mischief.

The building held the city's savings for ninety years. We kept the good parts — the vault, the stair, the light — and filled the rest with thirty-six rooms, a restaurant and a bar that takes its martinis seriously.

Eat & drink

Dinner in the old counting house.

Our kitchen cooks what the market gives it — North Sea fish, good butter, vegetables from the polder. The room is the original banking hall, so dinner comes with a little theatre.

The Vault Bar

Money used to sleep here. Now the martinis do.

Down the original bank stair, behind a two-tonne door, the vault is still lined in brass deposit boxes. We added a long bar, a short menu and a pianist on Fridays.

Wellness

A quiet hour, just for you.

The old strongroom below the lobby is now a small spa: one treatment room, a steam bath and a plunge pool. Book it for an hour and forget the city above.

  • Deep-tissue and jet-lag treatments
  • Private steam & plunge for two
  • In-room breakfast after, if you like

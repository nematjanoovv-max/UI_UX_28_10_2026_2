The studio

Small on purpose. Careful by nature.

Beautyness started with two chairs and a simple idea: beauty treatments should feel like being looked after, not being sold to. Twelve years later we are still a small team, still in the same light-filled room on the Singel.

  • One guest at a time, never double-booked
  • Gentle, fragrance-free products where possible
  • Appointments that start when they say they will

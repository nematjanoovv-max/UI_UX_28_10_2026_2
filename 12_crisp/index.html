The journal

Fewer, better, worn often.

We design eight pieces a season, not eighty. Each one is sampled, worn, washed and worn again before it goes into production. If it does not earn its place in a suitcase, it does not get made.

Our studio is in Amsterdam Noord. The fabric mill is in Portugal, the knitters are in Italy, and the patterns are cut two streets from here.
